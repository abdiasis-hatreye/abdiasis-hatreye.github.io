About me

A developer with a technician's mindset

I enjoy turning ideas into dependable software — and understanding technology all the way down to the hardware.

I'm a senior Computer Science student at Nugaal University in Laascaanood, Somalia. I build web systems with PHP, Laravel and MySQL, and mobile applications with Flutter and Dart, with a focus on tools that solve everyday problems for small businesses.

My hands-on work in mobile hardware and software repair, system installation and troubleshooting shapes how I build software: I think about the people who use it, the problems they actually face, and how to keep things running reliably.

I'm currently deepening my knowledge of networking, Linux, cybersecurity and cloud computing, and I'm looking for opportunities to learn from experienced teams, contribute to real-world projects and grow as a professional.
