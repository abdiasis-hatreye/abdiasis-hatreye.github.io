01 · About me

Building software, fixing problems

I'm a senior Computer Science student at Nugaal University in Laascaanood, Somalia. My studies have given me a foundation in programming and computing, and I enjoy turning that knowledge into software that solves real, everyday problems — especially for small businesses and local communities.

Alongside development, I have hands-on interest and experience in mobile phone hardware and software repair, operating-system installation, and troubleshooting computers. Working on devices directly has taught me to diagnose problems patiently and explain technical issues clearly.

I'm also exploring networking, Linux, cybersecurity, and cloud computing. I'm eager to keep learning, collaborate with teams — locally and internationally — and contribute to practical projects that make a difference.
